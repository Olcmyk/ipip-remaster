A Table Showing Which IPIP Items are Currently Assigned to Which Scales

Explanation: Please read before using the table.

Not all 3,320 IPIP items are currently scored on an IPIP scale, and some IPIP items are scored on more than one scale.

The link below leads to an Excel workbook containing a table constructed by Thomas Tedone that shows which items thus far have been assigned to which IPIP scales. He constructed this table of IPIP items and their associated trait labels by web-scraping hyperlinks found on the International Personality Item Pool website. The trait labels used in this table are those that appear on the IPIP web page that lists all IPIP scales organized by an alphabetical list of constructs. The table does not include IPIP items that are not currently scored and therefore have no association to any scale on this alphabetical list of constructs.

Thomas Tedone has developed a web application called Deep Personality, a comprehensive personality assessment based on the item assignment table. Deep Personality is not affiliated with the International Personality Item Pool. Thomas Tedone has generously agreed to share his table of item assignments for persons who can use such a resource.

Below are descriptions of the column headings in the item assignment table

  • instrument. The personality scale or inventory that existed prior to the creation of the IPIP and served as a target for a personality construct assessed by IPIP items. (Link to page describing IPIP scale construction procedures) Some instruments are listed by their name or an abbreviation, and some are listed by the instrument's author and publication date.
  • alpha. Cronbach alpha reliability coefficient of the scale on which the item is scored. Where more than one alpha has been reported, the values are separated by commas.
  • key. The keyed direction (positive or negative) of the item and its associated construct. 1 indicates a +keyed item and -1, a -keyed item. (Link to page describing how responses to +keyed and -keyed items are converted into IPIP scale scores)
  • text. The IPIP item.
  • label. The construct label from the alphabetical list of constructs
