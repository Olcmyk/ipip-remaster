Note. P+N/Items = Original number of positively and negatively keyed items. Alpha/1 = Reliability of original scale. Omitted = Number of positively and negatively keyed items omitted during scale refinement. Added = Number of positively and negatively keyed items added during scale refinement. P+N/2 = Number of positively and negatively keyed items in the refined scale. Alpha/2 = Reliability of the refined scale.


The IPIP representation of the Values in Action Inventory of Strengths (VIA-IS), or IPIP-VIA, is based on a preliminary version of the VIA-IS provided by Christopher Peterson in June 2001 and described in Peterson and Seligman (2003). The 240 items in the VIA-IS, plus 102 of Peterson’s items that were not included in the VIA-IS, were revised to give them the standard format of IPIP items (short phrases with no leading pronoun).  Since the 2001 version, the VIA Institute on Character has revised the VIA-IS several times; the Institute provides the more recent versions of the VIA-IS to researchers upon request. The Institute's current VIA-IS assessment suite is available at:  https://www.viacharacter.org/researchers/assessments/ .

 

Peterson, C., & Seligman, M. E. (2003). Character strengths before and after September 11. Psychological Science, 14, 381-384.
