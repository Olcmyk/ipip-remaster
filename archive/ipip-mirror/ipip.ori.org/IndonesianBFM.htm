Translation Procedure Provided by Hanif Akhtar
  1.  Translated the scale into Bahasa Indonesian by two professional translators who have a psychology education background. The translators translated the scale independently.
  2. Synthesized the two translations. Akhtar facilitated a discussion between the two translators on the best translation.
  3. Conducted a backward translation into English by a profesional translator and discussed the result with the developer of the scale.
  4. Commissioned a review of the semi-final translation by an expert on language, personality, and measurement.
  5. Tested translation with small sample quantities (n=5) to ensure that the items of the scale could be understood by the respondents.  
  6. Tested translation with a larger sample quantities (n=502) to estimate the reliability of the scales.

Reliabilities of the Scales

1.    Extraversion, Alpha=0.839,  Corrected Item-Total Correlations ranged from 0.419 - 0.677
2.    Agreeableness, Alpha=0.762,  Corrected Item-Total Correlations ranged from 0.292 - 0.596
3.    Conscientiousness, Alpha=0.811,  Corrected Item-Total Correlations ranged from 0.432 - 0.571
4.    Emotional Stability, Alpha=0.862,  Corrected Item-Total Correlations ranged from 0.395 - 0.662
5.    Intellect, Alpha=0.768,  Corrected Item-Total Correlations ranged from 0.269 - 0.645
