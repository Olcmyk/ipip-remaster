Note. P+N/Items = Original number of positively and negatively keyed items. Alpha/1 = Formerly reported, yet biased reliability estimate of original scale. Added (Added/2) = Number of positively and negatively keyed items added during scale refinement in 2004 (or in 2019). Omitted = Number of all positively and negatively keyed seminal items considered but omitted during scale refinement. P+N/3 = Number of positively and negatively keyed items in the revised short scale. Alpha/3 = Biased estimate of reliability of the revised short scale (Germany/UK). Omega/3 = Reliability of the revised short scale (Germany/UK). Retest/3 = Retest-reliability of the revised short scale across 2-3 weeks (Germany/UK).
